📋 Existing Situation
This Web GIS visualises the spatial relationship between mapped cropland areas, irrigation canals, drainage, rivers, and water bodies in Western Province. It is intended to support understanding of existing agricultural and water-related spatial conditions for planning purposes.
⚠ Public reports are unverified crowdsourced observations. Independent field verification is required before any formal planning use.
